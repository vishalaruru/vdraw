Vision AI: Whiteboard Sketch & Image to Diagram

Multimodal Gemini 2.5 Flash / 1.5 Flash Vision parsing
AI Module 1
1
What is that module?
An optical vision synthesis pipeline that ingests photos of physical whiteboards, architectural screenshots, or hand-drawn sketches and translates them into native, editable Draw.io shapes and connectors.
2
How to work on it?
Click AI ▾ → Upload Diagram Image. Drag and drop any image or paste from clipboard (⌘V). Select an enterprise color theme, click Convert Image to Diagram, and click Render on Draw.io Canvas.
3
Why it is for?
Engineers and teams after whiteboard brainstorming sessions who need to turn messy physical sketches into formal, presentation-ready system architecture diagrams.
4
What is motive?
Manual transcription from a photo to digital diagram takes 1 to 3 hours per diagram. Teams often abandon sketches because redrawing is tedious.
5
How it helps?
Reduces transcription time from hours to under 10 seconds. Preserves connection directions, labels, cloud roles, and database schemas automatically.
6
Why to use this?
Unlike optical OCR, our pipeline infers architectural relationships, categorizes cloud tiers (edge, compute, storage, cache), and supports offline heuristic fallbacks.

Latency, SLA & Availability Calculator

Composite availability (99.99%), downtime budgets, P95 latency, and bottleneck detection
Misc Module 2

What is that module?
A reliability engineering calculator that computes end-to-end composite availability ($A = \prod A_i$), maximum allowed downtime budgets, and P50/P95/P99 latency profiles across critical request paths.

How to work on it?
Click Misc ▾ → Latency & SLA. Inspect KPI cards (Composite SLA %, Yearly/Daily Downtime, P95 Tail Latency, and Primary Bottleneck). Select target benchmarks (99.9%, 99.99%, 99.999%) and copy the SLA report.

What is motive?

5
How it helps?
Demonstrates the exact mathematical impact of serial dependencies, highlights the slowest bottleneck node, and suggests decoupling via queues or caches.
6
Why to use this?
